Stage 1: Background and Demographics (START ZOOM RECORD)


* What is your current job title or academic role?
* How many years of professional experience do you have in your field?
* Demographics: familiarity with decision trees, python coding, AI agentic tools (1-5 scale from Low to High)
* (START OTTER AI RECORD)
* What is your workflow when working with sequence data? Are there any pain points you encounter when trying to extract biological insights from this data? 
   * (Potential follow up: what are the software tools you currently rely on for sequence analysis / what are they like to use?)
We will then explain the interface and task to users.
[a]


Stage 2: Think-Aloud Interface Exploration (5+3+3 MIN TIMER)


Progress through the major pages of the interface one by one and allow participants to explore them on their own.
* “What do you see on this page of the interface and how are you making sense of each component? What is confusing to you? Do you have any thoughts as you interact with the interface?”


Repeat for each main component:
1. Tree visualization canvas
   * Sensemaking/hypothesis hunting in data  
   * Potentially relevant literature dummy feature
      * Preprints or just published papers more important?
[b]
2. AI Agent
   * Advice submission/management
   * Asking questions – we want the agent to be able to coach experts through interpreting the decision tree, answer relevant questions about the data/biological context, etc.
